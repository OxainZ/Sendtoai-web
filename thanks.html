🎉 You're Pro. Two minutes to activate.

Thanks for supporting SendToAI. Here's exactly how to unlock everything.

Your license key is in your email

Lemon Squeezy sends it instantly to the address you used at checkout — subject line "Your SendToAI order". Check spam if you don't see it within a minute. It's also on your order receipt page.

1
Copy your license key from the email

It looks like XXXXXXXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXXXXXXXXXX.

2
Open VS Code → Command Palette

Press Ctrl+Shift+P (⌘+Shift+P on Mac) and type "SendToAI: Enter License Key". There's also an "Activate it here →" link at the bottom of the SendToAI sidebar panel.

3
Paste the key — done

You'll see "🎉 License activated!" and a PRO badge in the panel. Unlimited files, git diff mode, Fit to AI Window, and named presets are now on.

Using more than one computer?

Your license works on up to 3 machines — repeat the steps above on each. Moving to a new machine? Run "SendToAI: Deactivate License on This Machine" on the old one first to free the slot.

Something not working?

Email support@sendtoai.dev with your order email address and we'll sort it out fast. And if it's not for you, there's a no-questions 14-day refund.
